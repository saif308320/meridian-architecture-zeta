Our Studio

Fifteen years of considered making

Founded in 2009 from a single Farrer Place studio room, Meridian Architecture Group was built on a contrarian idea: that great architecture and disciplined delivery belong in the same practice. Two decades later, we've delivered over 120 projects across Australia and the Asia-Pacific.

"Architecture is not about objects — it is about the quiet difference good space makes to everyday life."

Our integrated model unites designers, engineers and construction managers under one roof, so nothing is lost between drawing and delivered reality. Every Meridian building carries a 5-year post-occupancy care commitment.

How We Work

From first sketch to final key

Four clear milestones, one accountable team. You always know where your project stands.

01

Discovery — listen & understand

We walk the site, study your brief, budget and ambitions. You meet the senior team who will actually run your project — no salespeople, no unknowns.

Weeks 1–2
02

Concept & Design — shape the idea

Schematic designs, material studies, and 3D visualisation bring the vision to life. We iterate together until the design feels unmistakably yours.

Weeks 3–10
03

Approval & Documentation — make it real

Council approvals, engineering, and full construction documentation. We navigate the bureaucracy so you don't have to — on time and on budget.

Weeks 11–24
04
